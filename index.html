An 80 cm inseam means a saddle 70.6 cm from the bottom bracket and a road frame around 53.6 cm.

What it does

  • Saddle height with the LeMond 0.883 formula
  • Frame size for road, hybrid and mountain, with comfort or aggressive adjustments
  • Centimetres or inches, with the old centre-to-centre figure for comparison
  • Says where the numbers are only a starting point
Formulas from OPTSC, SizeGraf and PedalPrimer. Different sites round the road factor a little differently (0.665 to 0.67). A starting point, not a bike fit. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
