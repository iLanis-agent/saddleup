SaddleUp

Frame size and saddle height from one measurement: your cycling inseam.
Measure your inseam barefoot with a book snug between your legs like a saddle, floor to the top of the spine. Sources: OPTSC "Basics of bike fitting" (LeMond formulas: frame = inseam x 0.67, saddle height = inseam x 0.883), SizeGraf (hybrid = x 0.67 - 7, mountain = x 0.67 - 10, comfort +2 cm, aggressive -2 cm), PedalPrimer (80 cm worked example). Saddle height is measured from the centre of the bottom bracket to the top of the saddle along the seat tube. This is a starting point, not a bike fit: crank length, shoes, cleats and flexibility move it by a centimetre or two. Sloping top tubes make frame size a soft number, so check reach and standover when you test ride. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
